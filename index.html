Company 01

WorkShop

AI Platform Engineering

WorkShop is an AI platform engineering tool. It gives early-stage founders the speed of "describe it, build it" tools like Lovable, backed by enterprise-grade guardrails, audit trails, an AI-powered IDE, and clear visibility into usage.

It handles the full software lifecycle - CI/CD, security, cost and operations automation, and even fixing known vulnerabilities - so founders spend less time managing infrastructure and more time building.

It's also built for freedom of choice: start with any cloud, language, or AI model, then switch between them later without losing control of your data or your code.

We built WorkShop first because every company we launch needs to ship software. Instead of stitching together GitHub, Jenkins, ArgoCD, Kubernetes, and a dozen other tools, WorkShop gives our founders one capable platform to build on - and it's the foundation every future SecretStartups company will run on.
